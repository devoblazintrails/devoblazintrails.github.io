The Right-Size Method

Consulting, sized to the problem.

Traditional consulting sells the engagement first and discovers the problem second. We reversed it. Diagnosis is a product, scope is a promise, and the exit is part of the plan.

01 · DIAGNOSE

Ground truth first

Most engagements open with a Health Check or Security Review. Two weeks max, a set price, and a shared picture of reality before anyone proposes anything.

02 · PRESCRIBE

A roadmap you own

The scored report and 90-day roadmap are yours to keep, whether or not we do the work. Some clients execute it internally. That is a fine outcome.

03 · BUILD

Scoped work, no surprises

Work ships in focused phases, scoped and agreed before it starts. When scope shifts, we pause, talk it through, and you decide. Nothing grows without your sign-off.

04 · EMPOWER

An exit, by design

Fractional support and training taper as your team's confidence grows. Success is your org running without us on speed dial.

"Right-sized" is not a tagline. It is a refusal: no discovery theater, no scope that grows in the dark, and no more consultant than you need.
